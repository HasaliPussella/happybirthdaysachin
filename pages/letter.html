SOME THINGS ARE BETTER WRITTEN DOWN

Dear Sachin,

I honestly don't think you realise how much your friendship has come to mean to me. This year brought so many people, little surprises and unexpected moments, but meeting you is one of the things I am genuinely, genuinely grateful for. Out of everyone I've met this year, you are one of the best people who came into my life — and I hope you never forget that.

Thank you for being the person you are. Thank you for the laughs, the random conversations, the silly moments and even the little things that might seem ordinary to you but somehow make my days better. Sometimes, the best people don't arrive with a big announcement. They just become part of your life, and one day you realise how much happier it feels with them around. That's what your friendship feels like to me.

I hope this new year of your life brings you everything your heart has been quietly wishing for. I hope you get opportunities that excite you, strength for the difficult days, peace when life gets overwhelming, and a thousand reasons to smile. I hope you always have people beside you who support you, believe in you and remind you how special you are — because you truly deserve that kind of happiness.

And no matter how busy life gets or how much things change, I hope we keep making memories. More random talks, more laughter until we can't breathe, more ridiculous moments that only we understand, and more stories we'll look back on someday. I'm really glad our paths crossed, and I'm even more glad I get to call you my best friend.

So, happy birthday, Sachin. Thank you for being one of the best parts of my year. I may not say it every day, but I appreciate you more than you know. Keep being you, keep chasing the things that make you happy, and please never doubt that you matter to the people lucky enough to know you.

With lots of love, always,

Your bestie ♥
